About PhytoSense

Built in the field, for the field.

We started PhytoSense after watching relatives lose part of a cotton crop to a monsoon that arrived three weeks late. Good data shouldn't be a luxury reserved for large farms.

Our mission

Put real-time soil and crop intelligence within reach of every smallholder farmer in India, regardless of income, literacy, or internet access — starting with a device that costs less than a season's fertiliser.

Our vision

A future where a farmer working one acre has the same quality of agronomic guidance as a large commercial estate — delivered in a voice they understand, on a device that never needs a data plan.
