Our Solution

Two devices, one simple job: tell you what your field needs.

PhytoSense pairs a rugged sensor that lives in the field with a handheld device that speaks. Together they turn raw soil signals into a plain-language recommendation, no reading required.

Meet the field unit and the voice assistant

The field sensor unit is planted directly in the soil, where it continuously reads moisture, temperature, and nutrient levels and transmits them over a low-power local link. The handheld voice device pairs with it, converting those readings into spoken guidance in the farmer's own language — press a button, hear an answer.

Both devices are built to survive dust, heat, and monsoon humidity, and neither one needs a data plan to work.

Feature breakdown

What each sensor reading actually gives you

Field sensor

Soil moisture sensing

A probe buried at root depth reports moisture levels every few minutes, catching both drought stress and waterlogging before they show up in the leaves.

Field sensor

Soil & ambient temperature

Temperature swings affect germination and nutrient uptake. PhytoSense tracks both soil and air temperature to flag conditions that call for shading, mulching, or a delayed sowing date.

Field sensor

NPK nutrient sensing

Built-in probes estimate nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium levels in the soil, so fertiliser gets applied where and when the crop actually needs it — not on a fixed calendar.

Handheld device

Voice assistant

Press one button and the handheld device reads out the day's advice in plain spoken language, no screen or literacy required. It answers common follow-up questions like "should I water today?" on the spot.

Both devices

Solar powered

A small integrated solar panel keeps both units charged through the growing season, so there's no battery to buy, no wall socket needed, and no downtime during power cuts.
