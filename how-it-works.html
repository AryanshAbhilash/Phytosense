How It Works

From soil to spoken advice, in four steps.

Every reading follows the same short path — designed to keep working even where mobile networks and electricity don't reach reliably.

1

Sensors collect data

The field unit's probes take moisture, temperature, and NPK readings from the root zone every few minutes and log them locally.

2

An onboard hub processes it

A low-power microcontroller in the field unit cleans and combines the readings, filtering out noise before anything is sent onward.

3

An on-device model generates advice

A lightweight recommendation model, trained on regional crop and soil data, converts the sensor readings into a specific action — water today, hold off, or apply nutrients.

4

The voice assistant speaks to the farmer

The handheld device announces the recommendation out loud in the farmer's chosen language, and can answer a couple of simple follow-up questions right there in the field.

<5s

from pressing the handheld device's button to hearing a spoken recommendation.
